1. CLO/PLO attainment will not be included in the proposed ERP functionality.
2. A separate notification subsystem will not be included in the proposed modules.
3. Announcements will remain an included feature of the system.
4. Fee management will be limited to maintaining fee records and tracking payment statuses.
5. A live payment gateway and actual payment processing will not be included in the project scope.
6. The prototype will use synthetic or authorized test data rather than unrestricted access to real university records.
7. The proposed system will be developed as a prototype and will not replace the university's existing production ERP.
8. Live integration with existing university systems will not be included unless specifically approved.
9. AI queries will retrieve information from UniSphere's own database through authorized backend services.
10. The AI system will not autonomously modify official grades, course registrations, or financial records.
11. AI access to ERP information will initially be limited to read-only queries.
12. GPA and CGPA calculations will be performed through deterministic backend logic rather than relying on LLM-generated calculations.
13. Grading scales, assessment weights, and other grading rules will need to be configured before they can be applied; no official rules will be assumed.
14. Course registration constraints will only be enforced when the relevant institutional rules have been confirmed and configured.
15. Finance Personnel will be added as a defined user role alongside Student, Faculty, and Administrator.
16. Role-based and record-level authorization will be implemented to ensure users can only access information permitted by their roles and ownership.
17. The RAG helpdesk will provide source references to support its answers and ensure responses are grounded in approved university documents.
18. The AI helpdesk will provide safe fallback responses when questions cannot be answered using sufficient supporting information.
19. The multi-agent workflow will be responsible for routing user requests to the appropriate agents, tools, and backend services.
20. Backend services will remain responsible for validating requests and enforcing access permissions; AI agents will not bypass these controls.
21. The proposed system modules will be updated to reflect the finalized project scope and included features.
22. The development task breakdown will include announcements and dashboards as part of the implementation work.
23. Project milestones will be updated to reflect the finalized ERP modules and multi-agent AI workflow.
24. The evaluation process will include retrieval relevance, answer correctness, source grounding, and permission enforcement.
25. Project limitations will clarify that the prototype uses synthetic or authorized test data and supports a restricted set of university workflows rather than a complete production ERP system.
